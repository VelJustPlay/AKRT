History was made as Axion Klyro Racing secured its first-ever race victory in the MRREL League.

VelJustPlay led the charge for the team's first MRREL win and his first career Grand Touring Masters victory. Right behind him, Bombah brought home P2 to complete a remarkable 1–2 finish.
